Title:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Natural Language-Based Restaurant Recommendation System 

Problem Statement:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Develop an intelligent restaurant recommendation system that accepts user requests in natural language and recommends restaurants based on inferred preferences such as location, cuisine, dining mode, budget, and restaurant characteristics.
The system should automatically extract user intent and preferences from free-text queries and identify the most relevant restaurants by combining restaurant metadata, ratings, popularity metrics, and customer review insights.

The recommendation engine should be capable of answering queries such as:
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Suggest good North Indian restaurants in Indiranagar under ₹1000."
"I want an authentic South Indian restaurant near Koramangala."
"Recommend places with table booking and great ambiance in Whitefield."
"Best biryani restaurants in HSR Layout."
"Good cafes in MG Road for online ordering."
"Restaurants serving authentic Italian food with high ratings."

The system should rank restaurants based on:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
User preference matching
Cuisine relevance
Location relevance
Budget compatibility
Restaurant ratings
Customer votes
Sentiment derived from customer reviews
Ambiance and authenticity indicators extracted from reviews

The objective is to provide personalized and context-aware restaurant recommendations without requiring users to enter structured filters.
